# Implementation phase

Invoke `research-plan-implement`. Require explicit approval of the current
`plan.md`. Execute its checklist one issue at a time, verify each issue, and mark
it complete only after verification passes. Continue until the approved plan is
finished or a genuine authorization or external blocker prevents progress. Do
not silently redesign the approved plan.
